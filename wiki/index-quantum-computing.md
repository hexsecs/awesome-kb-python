# Quantum Computing

- [qiskit](qiskit.md) — An IBM-backed quantum SDK for building, simulating, and running circuits on real quantum hardware.
- [qutip](qutip.md) — Quantum Toolbox in Python.
- [pennylane](pennylane.md) — A cross-platform library for quantum computing, quantum ML, and quantum chemistry.
- [cirq](cirq.md) — A Google-developed framework focused on hardware-aware quantum circuit design for NISQ devices.
