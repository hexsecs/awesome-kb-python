# AI and Agents / Speech

- [faster-whisper](faster-whisper.md) — A Whisper reimplementation on CTranslate2, up to 4 times faster than openai-whisper with less memory.
- [openai-whisper](openai-whisper.md) — A general-purpose automatic speech recognition model trained on 680k hours of multilingual and multitask supervised data.
- [gTTS](gtts.md) — Python library and CLI tool for converting text to speech using Google Translate TTS.
- [funasr](funasr.md) — Industrial-grade speech recognition toolkit with speaker diarization and emotion detection.
